## 3. Amendment 1 — the identity EVIDENCE_REFERENCE_DIFFERENCE is evaluated over

```text
PR          #58
merge       fbf1e1b0b18d3a4c8ebe9a012002a7b5db470017
branch      docs/ai-10-amendment-1-evidence-reference-identity
changed     docs/AI-10_CROSS_AGENT_REVIEW_EXECUTION_PLAN.md   +123 / -8
```

```text
PROBLEM (found by AI-10-B, recorded and not resolved by it)
    §9.3 defined the claim content digest as covering `evidence_refs`; §9.2 asked
    EVIDENCE_REFERENCE_DIFFERENCE to fire for a content digest present on both
    sides whose `evidence_refs` differ. Claims whose references differ do not
    share a content digest; claims that share one have equal references by
    construction. The frozen member was unreachable.

RESOLUTION (§9.3.1)
    claim content digest     statement + evidence_refs + targets, no claim_id
                             -> CLAIM_OVERLAP, CLAIM_UNIQUE_TO_LEFT/RIGHT, and the
                                claim-set comparison behind claims_digest
    claim statement digest   statement + targets, no claim_id, NO evidence_refs
                             -> EVIDENCE_REFERENCE_DIFFERENCE alone

Impact: no vocabulary change, no field, no dependency, no authority. `claims_digest`
and `material_digest` inputs and values are unchanged.
```
